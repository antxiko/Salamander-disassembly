# The bosses

The bosses are painted with **the ROM's characters and patterns** over the layout of an openMSX dump (`tools/jefes.py`).

The code of the eight bosses is in bank 11 (table `p01:6E89`). The
cartridge gives them no names: neither the English nor the Japanese texts
name any of them. The prophecy only speaks of «THE DEVIL» with his copper
shield, «THE HUGE SPHERE», «THE GREAT DEVIL» and his nucleus, and «THE FIVE
EYED GREEN DEVIL».

The names below are those of the [Gradius Wiki](https://gradius.fandom.com/wiki/Salamander_(MSX))
for the MSX version, and they are the same ones used by a 2009 Japanese
guide (galfned, on geocities.jp) that quotes the manual elsewhere; we have
not seen the manual. Each name goes with its boss by the stage (the ROM's
cards say stage 3 is EIONEUS, 4 LAVINIA and 5 KIERKE) and by its look: Dead
End has three cores, Metal Slave five, Outer Limits is the eye. For stage 7,
the wiki gives Venom and True Venom.
