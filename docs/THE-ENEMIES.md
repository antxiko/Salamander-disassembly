# The enemies

The enemies are painted with **the ROM's patterns** over what they do in openMSX (`tools/enemigos.py`).

There are 65 enemy types, each with a 7-byte record (`p01:6C5D`).

### The names

In the MSX version the enemies are named after heavy metal bands. The
cartridge does not carry them; these are the 43 from the
[Gradius Wiki](https://gradius.fandom.com/wiki/Salamander_(MSX)), with the
series enemy each one matches. The wiki gives no picture for each name, so
