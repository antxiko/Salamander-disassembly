The game

Seven stages. Stages 1 and 2 always come first; stages 3, 4 and 5, in whatever order the player picks; then stage 6. Stage 7 only exists with Nemesis 2 in the other slot.

Every picture on this page comes from tools/imagenes.py. The still screens of the intro, the radar and the endings are rebuilt from the bytes of the ROM and identical, pixel for pixel, to openMSX's VRAM at that moment; the enemies, the bosses, the weapon pieces and the items are drawn with the ROM's patterns. The animations are the VRAM and VDP registers of every frame of a game in openMSX, painted the way the TMS9918 paints them (tools/anima.py), one frame in three.

The intro

The whole intro, 32 seconds: the Konami logo on blue, the title with the menu (1PLAYER, EXCHANGE and DUALPLAY), THE SAGA CONTINUES, KONAMI PRESENTS and the title building up.

If nothing is pressed, the story follows (105 seconds): EMERGENCY, the warning from Latis, ZELOS-FORCE surrounding the planet, the two ships with their pilots and «CAN THEY RESCUE PLANET LATIS?», and the title again with «THE FANTASTIC SPACE ADVENTURE COMMENCES». Then the demo starts.

The still screens, each identical to the VRAM: Konami, the title, THE SAGA CONTINUES, KONAMI PRESENTS and the title on its own.

The stages and their order

The radar with the three planets.

After stage 2, the game shows the radar with EIONEUS, LAVINIA and KIERKE, and the player picks which one comes first: they are stages 3, 4 and 5, in whatever order he chooses (p10:988A). There is a clock: if it reaches zero, the game picks (p10:9A43). Stage 6 comes next. Stage 7, with Nemesis 2, comes before stage 6 (p10:8B7B).

Before each stage come the take-off from the base and a title card (p10:8A20):

The routes

Stage 1 whole, the way the game feeds it into the screen: it goes down twice through another map.

Stages 2, 3 and 6 scroll vertically; the others, horizontally (p02:8921). Each stage map is made of 4 x 4 character blocks (p13:6F4C), but the route is not a straight strip: in three stages the scroll stops and the screen moves through another map (p00:4BE6):

  • in stage 1, at scroll 0x11F the view goes down 24 rows through the map at p14:80FC, carries on horizontally and at 0x1FF goes down another 24;
  • in stage 4, at 0x1BF it goes down 24 rows through p14:815C;
  • in stage 6, which goes up, at 0x14F it moves 32 columns to the left and at 0x167 back to the right, through p14:818C.

The ship

The fourteen poses.

Two 16 x 16 sprites and fourteen poses (p09:A145-p09:A19F, patterns at p14:95F7): level, climbing and diving, the same with the shield in front, and as many again for the vertical stages. The shield takes the hits counted in IY+22 (p02:960B).

There are two ships: SABEL TIGER, flown by IGGY ROCK, and THRASHER, flown by ZOWIE SCOTT. The SELECTION screen at p12:B877 asks YES or NO to «PLAYER1 JOINT GAME».

The weapons

The four options, following the ship along the path it took.

At the bottom, the gauge has seven boxes (p00:5D8E). Each 0x29 capsule moves the chosen box along (p02:97C9) and the second button activates it (p02:8929): the box raises its weapon one level, with its routine from the table at p02:8967. On the right, a 5-character window says what is carried: a piece from the table at p14:8849, 35 bytes of pattern and one of colour, chosen by the box and its level (p00:5E52).

The 22 pieces, drawn from the ROM. Those of the seven boxes are identical to the ones the game paints in openMSX when picking up each capsule (tools/armas.py coteja).

Each link leads to the ship firing that weapon.

Boxes 2, 4 and 5 are the main weapon (IY+40: 1 NORMAL, 2 RIPPLE, 3 LASER, 4 SCREW, 5 METEO, 6 TWIN and 7 TRIPLE) and box 3 and the ARMING the panel weapon (IY+41: 1 to 3 the missiles and 4 the ARMING). The game only gives the second and third levels with both ships joined, in state 3 or 4 (p02:8995), when the window says PILOT or Co.PILOT. For the GIFs the weapon was set by hand in RAM (0xE340 and 0xE341); the options and the F.FIELD are activated as in the game. The ARMING goes wherever the controller is pushed (p03:B43F): with the ship still, it does not fire.

The capsules and the items

Items 1 to 6, drawn with the runs at p09:A331. Items 2 and 3 are identical, at 0 bytes, to the VRAM of two games in openMSX.

There are three capsules (p02:9670):

  • 0x29 moves the gauge box along;
  • 0x2A gives a bomb (IY+42, p02:9806) and the window says T.BOMB: while there are any, the button drops bombs instead of the panel weapon (p03:A962);
  • 0x35 adds one to IY+1B; at 15 it goes back to 0 and raises IY+10, and with IY+10 at 7 it gives an extra life (p02:974A).

The 0x29 and the 0x2A are in the sheet of the stage 4 enemies. The 0x35 has not shown up in any of the recorded games, and it is not drawn here.

Some enemies drop one of these items when destroyed (p01:7C36; with one player, 1 to 4). They are four characters in the scenery (p02:8B97) and give 30 points when picked up (p02:9909). Each one sets a clock (0xE630 the mode, 0xE631 the time) and its piece in the window (p02:991E):

The bosses

Boss 1, a brain with two tentacles.

The code of the eight bosses is in bank 11 (table p01:6E89). The cartridge gives them no names: neither the English nor the Japanese texts name any of them. The prophecy only speaks of «THE DEVIL» with his copper shield, «THE HUGE SPHERE», «THE GREAT DEVIL» and his nucleus, and «THE FIVE EYED GREEN DEVIL».

The two endings

The ending comes when boss 6 falls (p11:B58D). With Nemesis 2 in the other slot and 0xE30A set, it is the good one (p10:8F7F); otherwise, the other one.

The good ending: «CONGRATURATION! YOU ARE GREAT STAR FIGHTER».

Before it come «ZELOS FORCE HAS DISAPPEARED!!» and «...PEACE WILL RETURN TO LATIS AGAIN!!».

The other ending: VENOM shows up.

The CRUSH BLOW fails, VENOM shows up («HE DIED ONCE, BUT HE IS NOW REINCARNATED!!») and «THE CRUSH BLOW OPERATION TURNED OUT TO BE A FAILURE AFTER ALL». Both end with THE END and EXTEND PLAY? Y/N.

The texts

They are in bank 15, with a Japanese table and an English one chosen by byte 0x002B of the BIOS (p10:944C). The English ones, as they come (typos included):

  • «A WARNING SIGNAL FROM LATIS.», «ZELOS-FORCE SURROUNDING LATIS», «BIGIN CRUSH-BLOW OPERATIONS! SPACE FORCE!! GET SET!!», «HYPER SPACE FIGHTER», «SABEL TIGER / PILOT - IGGY ROCK», «THRASHER / PILOT - ZOWIE SCOTT», «CAN THEY RESCUE PLANET LATIS?»
  • between stages: «ASSESS ARMS SUPPLY SITUATION THEN DICIDE WITHIN TIME LIMIT.», «WE HAVE SET ALL OF THE CRUSH BLOW SYSTEMS.», «OK, SWITCH ON!!», «WAIT! PLEASE REMEMBER THE PREDICTION»
  • the prediction, in four chapters: the devil that came from the sphere and his copper shield, the huge sphere and the shining ball, the great devil and his nucleus, and the five-eyed green devil that can only be killed by the crystal.
